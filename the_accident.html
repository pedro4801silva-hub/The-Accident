The Accident

My legs were taken by a car, and my face was ruined by the fall into the ravine. My soul doesn't know whether to enter or stay behind, watching over me and protecting me from something. I am nothing more than an empty shell now, because my heart is already dead.

My arms are barely covered anymore, reduced to little more than bone and flesh. If this place—and a certain person's concern for their brother—didn't exist... Maybe I would still be whole. Maybe I would still be a normal human.

Now I am a monster. I need living flesh to survive.

Writing is the only way to bring my old life back... Maybe this place will listen to me.
